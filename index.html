Online Retail Sales Analysis

Python, SQL, Pandas, Matplotlib, Plotly

Problem: Find out which products, countries and customers bring the most sales.

  • Cleaned 541,909 records (kept 522,502, or 96.4%): removed duplicates, cancelled orders, non-product rows and invalid values.

PROJECT 02

Used Car Price Analysis (India)

Python, Pandas, SQL, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly

Problem: How fast do used cars lose value, and do automatic, diesel and dealer cars really cost more?

  • Cleaned 15,411 listings (kept 15,229). Removed 167 duplicates and fixed wrong values.
  • Used SQL window functions to compare prices by age, brand and model.

Finding 1: Cars lose about 11% of value per year (4.9% for Maruti Celerio, 16.3% for Mercedes E-Class).
Finding 2: Automatic cars cost 2.1x on raw medians, but only about 1.4x for the same model.
Limit: These are asking prices, not final sale prices.

Monthly sales

SELECT DATE_FORMAT(InvoiceDate,'%Y-%m') AS month,
       SUM(Quantity*UnitPrice) AS revenue
FROM sales
GROUP BY month
ORDER BY month;

Top 10 products

SELECT Description,
       SUM(Quantity*UnitPrice) AS revenue
FROM sales
GROUP BY Description
ORDER BY revenue DESC
LIMIT 10;
